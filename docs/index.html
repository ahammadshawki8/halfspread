Why the exit is the expensive half

Half the bid-ask, as a percentage of the option's mid price, across moneyness. Relative execution cost explodes as strikes go further out of the money - which is exactly where a delta-targeting screen puts you.

Widening through the session

Entry and exit spreads are identical at a single instant, so the claim is about time. These are the same strikes re-quoted through the day, measured rather than assumed.

Breach probability, counted not assumed

Black-Scholes assumes lognormal returns. Measured sessions are not: the tail is far fatter than the standard deviation implies, and the move still left in a session collapses through the day. Sizing takes the worse of the model and this record.
